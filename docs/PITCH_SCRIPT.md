## Pitch Structure Overview
- **Duration**: 9–11 minutes total (approx. 40 seconds per slide).
- **Core Narrative Arc**:
  1. *Hook & Friction* (Slides 1–2): Manual expense verification is slow, error-prone, and unscalable.
  2. *The Solution & Journey* (Slides 3–4): Automated pre-screening across 7 sequential layers before human review.
  3. *User Surfaces* (Slides 5–6): Lightweight Flutter mobile capture for field staff; split-screen evidence cockpit for managers.
  4. *Technical Deep-Dive* (Slides 7–10): OCR extraction vs document authenticity; 6 deterministic fraud algorithms; 0–100 risk scoring; strict AI boundaries (explanation vs control).
  5. *Cloud & Data Pipeline* (Slides 11–13): Multi-tier Fastify architecture; Supabase PostgreSQL + AWS serverless pipeline; frictionless join-code onboarding.
  6. *Outcomes & Strategic Close* (Slides 14–15): Verifiable operational improvements; 5-phase roadmap from verification to enterprise ERP integration.
