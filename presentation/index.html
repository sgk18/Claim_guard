Deterministic Math Engine

OCR is only the beginning: 6 verification signals.

+40 PTS SIGNAL 1

Perceptual Duplicate Hash

Hamming distance ≤ 5 flags exact, cropped, or re-photographed duplicate bills across employees.

+25 PTS SIGNAL 2

GSTIN Luhn Checksum

Validates 15-character Indian tax ID structure, state prefix codes (01–38), and ISO 7064 checksum digit.

+20 PTS SIGNAL 3

Amount Anomaly Baseline

Flags claims exceeding 2.0x the employee's historical category average (sample size ≥ 3).

+20 PTS SIGNAL 4

Merchant Category Mismatch

Taxonomy cross-referencing detects dining, hotel, or personal expenses misclassified as vehicle fuel.

+15 PTS SIGNAL 5

Policy Limit Violation

Enforces single-claim organizational spending caps and mandatory tax invoice thresholds.

+15 PTS SIGNAL 6

Date & Timing Anomaly

Detects future dates (invariant violation) or stale expense receipts submitted >90 days after purchase.

Risk Prioritization

Turn multiple signals into a decision-ready risk view.

5 EVIDENCE-BASED STATES
VERIFIED SCORE: 0–24

Valid GSTIN, clear OCR (>90%), zero fraud flags. Auto-clear or one-click approval.

LIKELY VALID SCORE: 0–24

Minor non-critical warning, slight date deviation, valid math. Standard approval queue.

REVIEW REQUIRED SCORE: 25–49

Amount anomaly or policy threshold breach needing explicit manager sign-off.

SUSPICIOUS SCORE: 50–74

Duplicate receipt hash match or invalid tax checksum. Flagged for audit investigation.

UNABLE TO VERIFY OCR LOW CONFIDENCE

Blurred camera capture, illegible merchant ink. Prompts employee for verified re-upload.

Boundary of Intelligence

AI assists the review. It does not replace it.

WHAT AI CAN DO (EXPLANATION LAYER)
Summarizes Signals: Translates raw perceptual hashes & anomaly ratios into a 2-sentence executive brief.
Actionable Inspection Tips: Recommends specific questions for manager clarification.
Taxonomy Normalization: Categorizes ambiguous merchant names into policy buckets.
Accelerates Triage: Provides context so managers make faster, more confident decisions.
WHAT AI DOES NOT DO (STRICT CONTROLS)
Zero Autonomous Approvals: AI never approves or disburses company expense funds.
Zero Autonomous Rejections: AI never rejects a claim without human manager confirmation.
No Policy Overrides: AI cannot alter spending limits, thresholds, or GST requirements.
Immutable Audit Records: AI cannot touch, mutate, or delete historical database rows.
