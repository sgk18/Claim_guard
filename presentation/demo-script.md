# ClaimGuard Live Demonstration Script

**Objective:** Walk an audience or evaluation panel through a live, end-to-end demonstration of ClaimGuard in 4 to 5 minutes, demonstrating synchronized interaction between the Employee Mobile Application and the Manager Operations Dashboard.

---

## Pre-Flight Checklist
- [ ] ClaimGuard Backend running on `http://localhost:3001` (or local port).
- [ ] ClaimGuard Web Application running on `http://localhost:3000` (or local port).
- [ ] Flutter Mobile Application running on Android Emulator or Chrome (`flutter run -d chrome`).
- [ ] Test receipt images ready:
  1. `clean_fuel_receipt.jpg` (Clean valid Indian Oil bill, ₹3,850, valid GSTIN).
  2. `duplicate_fuel_receipt.jpg` (Exact image hash match of previous claim).
  3. `dining_as_fuel_receipt.jpg` (Restaurant receipt tagged as Fuel category).

---

## 19-Step Demonstration Sequence

| Step # | Action | Surface / View | Spoken Talk Track | Visual Cues to Highlight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | Open Employee App | Mobile App (`/employee` or Flutter client) | "We begin on the mobile client used by our field employee, Rahul." | Clean mobile UI, organization badge. |
| **02** | Connect to Organization | Onboarding Screen | "Rahul connects to his company using a 6-character join code generated by his manager." | Instant connection without Google/social login. |
| **03** | Initiate Receipt Capture | Employee Home -> Camera | "He taps the camera button to submit a daily fuel expense." | Camera viewfinder guide with orange alignment corners. |
| **04** | Show OCR Ingestion | Processing State | "The moment he snaps the photo, the backend sends the encrypted image for OCR parsing." | Animated processing indicator: 'Reading receipt...'. |
| **05** | Inspect Extracted Data | Extracted Summary Card | "Within seconds, AWS Textract parses the vendor, date, amount, and GSTIN with high confidence." | Pre-populated fields: Indian Oil Corp, ₹3,850, 19 Sep 2026. |
| **06** | Demonstrate Validation | Inline Review Form | "Rahul verifies that the total matches his fuel dispenser slip. The math and date are valid." | Green 'Verified' badge next to high-confidence fields. |
| **07** | Trigger Suspicious Signal (Demo Branch) | Duplicate Upload | "Now let's see what happens if an employee accidentally submits the same receipt photo twice." | Re-upload same image or choose duplicate test scenario. |
| **08** | Show Immediate Risk Feedback | Mobile Review Card | "The server computes the perceptual image hash and detects that this image already exists in our system." | Perceptual hash collision indicator (`D_H = 0`). |
| **09** | Submit Claim | Submit Button | "Rahul submits the claim. The mobile app immediately transitions to real-time status tracking." | Claim ID generated (`CLM-2026-0919`), status: `SUBMITTED`. |
| **10** | Open Manager Dashboard | Web Portal (`/manager`) | "Now let's switch over to Priya, our finance operations manager, looking at her claims queue." | Live KPI bar (Total, Pending, High Risk, Fraud Prevented). |
| **11** | Inspect Pending Queue | Claims Table | "Priya sees the incoming claim categorized by its calculated risk tier." | Amber/Red risk badge on the duplicate claim. |
| **12** | Open Claim Split Review | Claim Detail Drawer | "Clicking the claim opens our split-screen review workspace." | Left: Scanned receipt image. Right: Structured verification evidence. |
| **13** | Inspect High-Res Receipt | Left Pane (Image Viewer) | "On the left, Priya can zoom in and inspect the original receipt with OCR bounding boxes." | Bounding boxes highlighting Total and GSTIN. |
| **14** | Review OCR Extraction | Right Pane (Fields Card) | "On the right, all extracted key-values are presented in clean, structured typography." | Clear data table: Merchant, Amount, GSTIN, Date, Category. |
| **15** | Examine Fraud Signals | Fraud Signals Card | "ClaimGuard highlights the exact triggered rule: Perceptual Duplicate Hash Match." | High-severity flag: `DUPLICATE_RECEIPT` (+40 points), original claim link. |
| **16** | Evaluate Risk Score & AI Summary | Risk Card & AI Narrative | "The score sits at 65/100 (HIGH Risk). Claude on Bedrock explains: 'Image matches Claim #842 submitted on 28 Aug by same employee'." | Circular risk gauge, 'REVIEW REQUIRED' chip, plain-English summary. |
| **17** | Manager Decision Action | Action Buttons | "Priya has full control. She clicks 'Reject Claim' and enters a brief audit note: 'Duplicate submission of August fuel bill'." | Confirmation modal, mandatory notes input field. |
| **18** | Verify Immutable Audit Trail | Audit History Timeline | "The rejection and note are committed to the immutable PostgreSQL audit log with Priya's user ID and timestamp." | Audit timeline node: `REJECTED by Priya Sharma at 19:38 UTC`. |
| **19** | Return to Employee Mobile | Mobile App Status Screen | "Back on Rahul's phone, the claim status instantly flips from 'Pending Review' to 'Rejected: Duplicate Submission'." | Real-time push notification card, updated status badge. |

---

## Demonstration Wrap-Up (15s)
"In under four minutes, you have seen the complete ClaimGuard loop: frictionless mobile capture, automated OCR extraction, deterministic duplicate detection, explainable AI synthesis, manager split-screen review, and full bidirectional audit synchronization. That is ClaimGuard in action."
